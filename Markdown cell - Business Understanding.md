# Bean there Done That – Storytelling with Data

## 1. Business Understanding

### Company Context

Bean there Done That is a small coffee roaster based in the Netherlands. The company produces approximately 1,000 tonnes of coffee per year and has less than 1% of the Dutch market. It sells specialty coffee at a premium price, mainly through e-commerce and partly through B2B customers.

The company experienced difficulties managing growth in previous years. At the beginning of 2023, management decided not to accept new customers in order to create a more stable operating situation.

The CEO wants to know:

**Are we ready to grow next year?**

### Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to evaluate the current performance of the company's operations and identify potential improvement opportunities and growth roadblocks.

The analysis will focus on:
- sourcing;
- production;
- warehouse operations;
- e-commerce sales;
- B2B sales;
- delivery performance.

### Main Business Question

**Is Bean there Done That operationally ready to grow next year, and which operational improvements are needed before expanding?**

### Sub-questions

- Does the company maintain sufficient raw material inventory?
- Are there signs of waste or expiration?
- How efficiently are the roasting machines being used?
- How many production batches are rejected?
- How much finished product is stored?
- How are sales developing over time?
- Which products generate the highest demand?
- Does delivery performance change when sales volume increases?
